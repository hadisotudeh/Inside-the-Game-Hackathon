Each completed open-play pass or carry adds end-location xT minus start-location xT, using Karun Singh’s pretrained 12×8 grid recommended by socceraction. Each action adds its signed xT; its influence halves every 15 seconds of match time. Plot the smoothed home-minus-away difference, resetting each half. Shots, failed passes and restarts are excluded. This measures attacking ball progression, not win probability or defensive value. Missing movement coordinates mark the remainder of that half incomplete. The chart rescales as the match progresses.
