The score tells you what's happened. But who's actually creating the danger? That's where Touchline comes in.

Think of it as a companion for fans watching live TV or a stream. Here, we're using a pitch view instead of broadcast footage.

Each event tells us something: who made the pass, where a shot came from, what happened next. This demo uses recorded Premier League events. A live version would connect to a licensed feed.

Watch the clock and the stats. They update as each event comes in. We've sped up the replay so you can see it in action.

Want a closer look? Pick a shot to see who took it and how good the chance was. The momentum chart tracks attacking moves. And the passing network shows who's connecting with whom.
